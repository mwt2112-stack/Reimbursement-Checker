Not allowed on grants

Always unallowable on federal awards under the Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200). Your specific award can restrict more - your grants administrator has the final word.
✕ Never allowed - no exceptions
✕
Alcohol§200.423
Always unallowable, even at a business meal. Split it out and charge it to a non-sponsored account.
✕
Entertainment & social events§200.438
Shows, sporting events, amusement - and the meals, travel, or lodging tied to them - unless written into the award for a programmatic purpose.
✕
Fines, penalties & late fees§200.441
Anything resulting from a violation of law or regulation.
✕
Fundraising & donations§200.442
Soliciting gifts, endowment drives, contributions to other organizations.
✕
Lobbying§200.450
Costs to influence legislation, elections, or government decisions.
✕
Personal-use goods & services§200.445
Anything for personal benefit, even if you'd report it as taxable income.
✕
Social, dining & country club dues§200.454
Memberships in these clubs are never chargeable.
✕
Alumni & commencement activities§200.424 · .429
Alumni relations and graduation/convocation costs.
✕
General advertising & PR§200.421
Only narrow exceptions (recruiting project staff, procuring goods for the award) are allowed.

Foreign travel
Usually needs sponsor prior approval - and federal funds require a U.S. carrier (Fly America) or an exception.
!
Equipment $10,000+ per unit§200.439
Allowable but generally needs prior approval unless already in the budget.

Costs outside the award period or over budget
Pre-award and off-budget spending need sign-off before you commit.
